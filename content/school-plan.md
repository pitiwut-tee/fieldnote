## Science weighting for the first-round exam (estimate, not official)
KruNee counted the 31 sub-topics in the MWIT 2570 announcement annex:
physics + electricity/electronics ≈ 35%, biology ≈ 23%, earth & space ≈ 19%, chemistry ≈ 13%,
science-technology-society ≈ 10%. (Another KruNee page gives bio 25 / chem 20 / physics 30 / earth 25.)
Use this to balance science sets over each month: physics/electricity (s12–s18, t1–t3) should be the largest
share of science content outside the current school topic; chemistry should not dominate just because the school
is teaching it now.

## Important consequence
The exam is mid-November of ม.3, so **ม.3 term-2 topics (s5, s6, s17, s11) will not have been taught at school
before the exam**. Teach them through the app: introduce at level 1 during the March–May 2570 break, then
lessons and probes through ม.3 term 1 (June–September 2570). Do the same for ม.3 math topics.

## Gaps the tutoring material shows in our content
- Dilution and mixing of solutions: C1V1 = C2V2, % by mass vs % by volume, using density to convert.
- Body systems (s9, already learned at school): recall-heavy exam items (respiration, excretion, nervous,
  reproduction, circulation) — good probe topic.
- Heating/cooling curves of pure substances vs mixtures (s2) appear often.
- Ignore tutor topics beyond ม.1–ม.3 (half-life, naming ionic compounds, PCR detail): not in the announcement.
